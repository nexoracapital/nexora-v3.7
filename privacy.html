🔐 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexora Community collects, uses and protects basic user information.

Information We Collect

Nexora Community may collect basic information when users use different services on the website, such as profile details, contact information, support requests, marketplace activity, school system details, quiz activity and login information.

  • Name and contact information when submitted by the user
  • Email address when using Gmail login or support features
  • Marketplace buyer/seller information
  • Support messages and feedback
  • School, quiz and educational activity data

How We Use Information

We use user information to provide website features, manage accounts, improve services, process marketplace actions, respond to support requests and maintain the Nexora Community system.

  • To operate community, marketplace, school and educational tools
  • To manage user profiles and support tickets
  • To improve website experience and security
  • To display relevant updates and messages

Google Login

Some parts of Nexora Community may use Google/Gmail login for secure access. When a user logs in, we may receive basic account information such as email address, display name and user ID for account management purposes.

Cookies and Analytics

Nexora Community may use basic analytics, cookies or similar technologies in the future to understand website traffic, improve services and support advertising features such as Google AdSense.

Data Protection

We try to protect user information using reasonable technical and administrative measures. However, no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

Nexora Community may use third-party services such as Firebase, Google login, Vercel hosting, Google Analytics, Google Search Console and advertising services. These services may process data according to their own policies.

User Responsibility

Users should not share sensitive personal information, passwords, payment secrets or private documents through public forms unless required and trusted.
