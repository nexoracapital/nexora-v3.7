Nexora School OS Structure:
School Manager will manage school profile, teachers, students, classes, attendance, online tests, result sheets, admissions and merit lists. Teacher and Student dashboards will work through secure Firestore records generated by School Manager. Tuition Academy remains a separate public learning/tuition system with Gmail login.
